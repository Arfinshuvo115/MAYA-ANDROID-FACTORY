# Maya AI Android App

Maya AI is a native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose Material 3 Android companion app featuring:

- **Bilingual Chat (Bangla & English)**: Multi-persona assistant (`Maya Core`, `Architect`, `Muse`, `Strategist`) with collapsible thought-process cards, rich Markdown rendering, Photo Picker attachments, and clipboard copy.
- **Bangla Voice Studio**: On-device Android Text-to-Speech supporting `bn-BD` and `bn-IN` accents, customizable voice pitch/rate profiles, delivery tones, and quick Bangla phrases.
- **AI Studio Workflows**: Structured workflows (*Smart Summarizer*, *Code Architect*, *Idea & Copy Forge*, and *Vision Inspector*) with adaptive single- or dual-pane layout and one-tap **Continue in Chat**.
- **On-Device History & Preferences**: Persistent local conversation threads and studio outputs with search, pinning, and DataStore preferences.
